# Summary
Profiles of primary users interacting with OnboardIQ.

# Key Concepts
1. **The New Hire (SWE - Juan)**: Needs quick developer setup, clear documentation links, and manager contacts. Frustrated by delayed logins and unclear checklists.
2. **The Engineering Manager (Sarah)**: Wants automated tracking of setup completion. Needs to easily assign technical mentors.
3. **The HR Coordinator (Elena)**: Oversees contract compliance, policy training, and basic corporate onboarding. Wants a centralized status dashboard.
