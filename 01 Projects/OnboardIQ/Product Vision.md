# Summary
OnboardIQ is an enterprise employee onboarding SaaS designed to tackle operational inefficiencies, administrative delays, and knowledge retention issues faced by new hires at Hewlett Packard Enterprise (HPE).

# Key Concepts
- **Frictionless Integration**: Automate the standard HR stack to provisioning pipeline.
- **Dynamic Learning Paths**: Context-aware modules depending on employee role (SWE vs. Sales).
- **Interactive Checklists**: Multi-stakeholder progress bars visible to HR, manager, and employee.

# Practical Applications
- Redundant form filing eliminated by dynamic profile synthesis.
- Role-specific system setups automated (Git tokens, LDAP, IDE profiles).
