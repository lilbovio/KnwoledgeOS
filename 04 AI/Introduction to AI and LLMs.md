# Summary
Fundamental concepts of AI, ML, DL, and GenAI. Explains the differences between ANI, AGI, ASI, and how LLMs are trained and tokenized.

# Key Concepts
- **Hierarchy of AI**: AI > ML > DL > GenAI > LLMs.
  - *ML*: Learns from data patterns, not hand-written rules.
  - *DL*: ML using deep neural networks.
  - *GenAI*: DL oriented to produce new content.
  - *LLMs*: GenAI specialized in language.
- **Levels of Intelligence**:
  - *ANI (Artificial Narrow Intelligence)*: Solves a specific task/domain (e.g., Claude, Gemini). Everything today is ANI.
  - *AGI (Artificial General Intelligence)*: Comparable to a human in any cognitive task, transfers learning across domains. Speculated for 2030.
  - *ASI (Artificial Super Intelligence)*: Surpasses humans in everything. Does not exist yet.
- **Types of AI by Mode**:
  - *Assistant*: Passive, reactive.
  - *Copilot*: Lives inside your tool, suggests while you work.
  - *Agent (2025)*: Executes actions, calls APIs, browses web autonomously to reach a goal.
  - *Superagent*: A Director-Of-Agents that coordinates specialists.
- **How an LLM Learns**:
  - *Pretraining*: "Learning the pattern". Happens once, costs millions, takes months.
  - *Post-training*: Supervised Fine Tuning (SFT) with good examples, Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback (RLHF), safety rules.
  - *Inference*: "Using the trained model". Costs cents, takes seconds.
- **Tokenization**:
  - A token is the minimum unit an LLM processes.
  - *Rule of thumb*: In English, ~4 characters per token (0.75 words). Spanish consumes 1.5x - 2.0x more tokens.
