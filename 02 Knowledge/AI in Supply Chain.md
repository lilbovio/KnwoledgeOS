# Summary
How AI is applied to Supply Chains, framing them as distributed systems requiring prediction and optimization at scale.

# Key Concepts
- **Supply Chain as a Distributed System**: Composed of nodes and flows, facing latency, failures, and partial information. Requires prediction + optimization at planetary scale.
- **The Bullwhip Effect**: Small fluctuations in retail demand cause progressively larger fluctuations in wholesale, distributor, and manufacturer demand.
- **Predict**: Guessing the future for millions of products. Reducing forecast error is highly valuable.
- **Position (The Optimizer Dilemma)**: "How much and where?". Warehouses act as a reinforcement learning playground. Computer vision is used for quality, counting, and chaos control.
- **Move (Vehicle Routing Problem)**: Half the costs occur in the last few kilometers.
- **Adapt**: Seeing the shock before it arrives.
- **Reason**: Transitioning from mere optimization to reasoning using GenAI.
