# Summary
PowerBI's evolution from a visualization tool to a complete analytics and data engineering platform, and the mindset of a Data Analyst.

# Key Concepts
- **PowerBI Evolution**: It's no longer just for visualization. It is a complete analytics and data engineering platform that transforms raw data into actionable insights for decision making.
- **Nearshoring Market**: Mexico has become the #1 market for data engineers and analysts in the region.
- **AI vs Data Analyst**: 
  - AI tells you *how* to do it.
  - A Data Analyst *knows* how to do it and has the context of the business functionalities.
- **Mindset**:
  - Go beyond just representing data and making reports.
  - The artistic side of data.
  - The freedom that comes from efficiency.
  - Continuous learning as an engine of freedom.
